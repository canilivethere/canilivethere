## 1. What this site is

CanILiveThere collects and verifies information about relocating to
places around the world — visa routes, costs, property mechanics,
risks — and shows how those facts land for different kinds of person.

It is a reference work assembled from public sources. Each fact carries
its sourcing so you can go and check it yourself. That is the intended
way to use the site: as a starting point that tells you where to look,
not as a final answer.

## 2. What it is not

**Not legal advice. Not immigration advice. Not tax or financial advice.
Not a substitute for a professional.**

Immigration rules change without notice, are applied inconsistently by
individual officials, and frequently depend on facts about you that this
site does not know. A route shown here as open may be closed, suspended,
repriced, or refused in your particular case. Costs move. Sources go
stale between checks.

Before you spend money, book travel, resign from anything, or submit an
application, verify with the relevant government authority or a qualified
professional in that jurisdiction.

## 3. Accuracy, and how we handle being wrong

We verify facts against their sources and re-check them, and we record
when a figure was last confirmed. We still get things wrong. Some facts
carry an explicit confidence marker or a stated gap; those are honest
signals, not decoration — take them seriously.

The site is provided **as is**, with no warranty of accuracy,
completeness or fitness for any purpose. To the fullest extent the law
allows, we are not liable for any loss arising from your use of it or
from reliance on anything in it.

**If you find something wrong, tell us** — hello@canilivethere.info. A
corrected figure is worth more to us than an unchallenged one.
