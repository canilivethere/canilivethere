## If you changed something

Say so. A reader needs to be able to tell your figures from ours —
especially if one of them is wrong. Something like:

> Based on data from [CanILiveThere](https://canilivethere.info)
> (CC BY 4.0), modified: figures converted to USD and rounded.

Do not imply we reviewed or endorse your version.

## Please keep the sourcing

Not a licence condition — a request. Most rows carry a `source_url`, a
date, and often a confidence marker or an explicit gap. That provenance
is the actual work: without it a figure is just a number someone typed.
Stripping it leaves your readers unable to check anything, and leaves us
unable to help when a figure turns out to be stale.

## What is here

One file per country, plus the cross-cutting exports. Each row carries
its own scope, sourcing and date. Files are JSON Lines — one JSON object
per line, so you can stream them without parsing the whole file.

`js/worldmap-data.js` in the site root is **not** covered by this notice
— it is third-party and share-alike. See `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.
