Overview
  • Alcohol law/culture
    Normal, unrestricted, sold/consumed openly nationwide despite a ~57% Muslim-background population; legal drinking age 18
  • Cannabis law
    Illegal recreationally; possession beyond a small amount, cultivation, sale, or distribution can carry 5-10 years imprisonment
  • Foreigner-targeted crime schemes (express kidnapping, ATM-cloning, etc.)
    Not yet researched
  • Internet — national fixed-broadband average
    93.69 Mbps download (46.18 Mbps upload)
  • Pet import (dog and cat) — rabies titer / quarantine requirement
    No mandatory quarantine if requirements met; microchip first, THEN rabies vaccination 21+ days before entry (sequencing matters); no titer test required from any country
  • Private health insurance cost — local Albanian plans
    450–760 €/year
  • Private health insurance enrollment age ceiling
    60 (standard/silver/gold tiers); 79 (travel-oriented 'Live and Travel in Albania' package) years
  • Public healthcare system access for foreigners
    Gated on local employment / social-insurance contribution, not residency alone — a non-employed foreign resident (retiree, remote-worker, passive-income) reads as functionally private-only for planned/non-emergency care
  • Unique Permit — health-insurance proof as a hard document requirement
    Not yet researched
  • Self-employed/freelancer income tax rate — 0% incentive through 2029
    0% income tax on annual gross income up to ALL 14,000,000 (~€120,700-135,000 depending on exchange-rate assumption) for registered self-employed individuals (Person Fizik Tregtar), in force through December 31, 2029; 5% net-profit tax is the standard rate that applies after the incentive expires
  • Self-employed mandatory social + health insurance contribution
    14900 ALL/month (~€135-143), mandatory regardless of actual income level
  • Self-employed registration mechanism
    Register as Person Fizik Tregtar via QKB (National Business Center), online through e-Albania with minimal documentation; invoicing requires fiskalizimi (a digital certificate via NAIS plus invoicing software, ~ALL 5,000-15,000/month)
  • Average monthly wage (national)
    90119 ALL/month (Q1 2026, ~$925-975 depending on exchange rate)
  • Median monthly income (national)
    40000 ALL/month (~$489)
  • Climate — Mediterranean coastal
    Mediterranean coastal; hot dry summers (Jul avg 31°C day/19°C night), cold wet winters (Jan avg 12°C day/4°C night); distinct seasonal swing with Dec wettest (12 rain days avg)
  • Distance to emergency care
    ~45 minutes by ferry
  • Emergency care — free at point of service — Spitali Sarandë (Petro Nako)
    Yes (locals and tourists)
  • Internet speed (Sarandë-specific)
    47 Mbps download (41 Mbps upload, from 2,535 tests; range 43-49 Mbps download; Vodafone 4G/5G 20-50 Mbps)
  • Kidnapping/cartel-extortion risk specifically targeting foreigners
    Not yet researched
  • Local hospital capability — Spitali Sarandë (Petro Nako)
    Public hospital — emergency (Urgjenca), general consultation, minor surgery, observation; limited capacity, not equipped for serious/complex cases
  • Population
    15000–30000 (commonly cited ~20,000) people
  • Private clinic capability
    Intermedica Clinic, Doctors General Clinic (24/7 emergency line) — consultations and non-critical care only; no hospital-tier private facility in town
  • Where the medevac line sits
    Essentially any serious emergency — strong, repeated expat-community recommendation is the ~45-minute ferry to Corfu General Hospital, Greece, or air evacuation, rather than relying on the local hospital
  • Ksamil beaches — proximity and quality
    ~15 km south of Sarandë (~20-25 min drive); Blue Flag-quality, white-sand beaches and small islands within Butrint National Park; frequently compared to Caribbean/Maldives-tier water clarity
  • Blue Eye Spring (Syri i Kaltër) — karst natural water feature
    A karst spring roughly 40-50 min drive from Sarandë; water stays 8-12°C year-round even in summer heat; a named, established regional natural landmark
  • Butrint National Park — UNESCO World Heritage Site
    ~18 km south of Sarandë; forested peninsula and lagoon system, UNESCO World Heritage Site; ancient Greek/Roman/Byzantine/Venetian ruins within a real, protected natural setting
  • Local income opportunity / freelance market
    Tourism/hospitality services primary employment; no active tech-outsourcing or coworking ecosystem documented for Sarandë proper; national self-employed 0% tax incentive through 2029 applies but location lacks Tirana's visible remote-work infrastructure

Red flags

The hard truths, stated plainly — real risks, sitting right next to everything that's actually going well.

  • Electoral/political polarization
    'Persistent political polarization, mistrust and political control over electoral administration,' a possible shift toward broader 'crisis of the political system' in 2026
  • EU accession status
    All 6 negotiating clusters/33 chapters opened, none closed; EU's own tracking doesn't foresee closing judiciary/fundamental-rights clusters given current conditions
  • EU institutional read on rule of law
    European Parliament report + European People's Party (EPP) statement (Feb 2026) named ongoing judicial pressure and persistent corruption as active concerns
  • No single-moment regime-change/currency-crisis event
    No (checked, not found)
  • Seismic risk
    November 2019 Durrës earthquake (M6.4) killed 51, significant coastal damage — recent memory, not ancient history
  • Tirana mayor Erion Veliaj — corruption prosecution
    Arrested by Albania's Special Court Against Corruption and Organised Crime (SPAK) on corruption/money-laundering charges, ~110M lek (~$1.15M) in illicit income alleged; Constitutional Court rejected appeal, remains in pre-trial detention
  • General safety impression
    Moderate (4/10 per cited source's own scale); petty-crime/scam-focused, not violent-crime-focused; busy central/seafront areas fine in the evening, isolated uphill or shoreline routes discouraged alone late at night
  • Named actual risk texture
    Petty theft, inflated tourist-season prices, aggressive driving, alcohol-fueled nightlife incidents in summer
  • Pollution Index (Numbeo)
    51.72 Numbeo Pollution Index ('Moderate')
