A stock held on deposit, not an annual flow. € available on deposit (12 × minimum monthly pension for a 1-year permit; 6 × at the visa stage) — a one-time balance, NOT €/year of income

WHAT PRIMARY LAW ACTUALLY SAYS: the route is Чл. 24, ал. 1, т. 10 ЗЧРБ — the Bulgarian MFA's own type-D document page (https://www.mfa.bg/bg/5686, fetched 2026-08-26) states the ground as 'пенсионно осигурени и разполагат с достатъчно средства за издръжка в страната', and the pensioner-specific document it names is a Bulgarian bank document certifying an account exists to receive the pension transfers — with NO amount attached to the pensioner ground itself. The amount comes from the general sufficiency clause, which the MFA page, the Council of Ministers' administrative-services register (https://iisda.government.bg/adm_services/services/service_provision/21815, service 'Разрешаване на продължително пребиваване на чужденци', fetched twice with independently-worded prompts 2026-08-26) and MVR's own migration page all render as means of subsistence 'в размер, не по-малък от минималната месечна работна заплата, минималната стипендия или минималната пенсия за страната, за срока на пребиваване' — i.e. a per-month benchmark multiplied by the months of residence, which is where '12×' comes from for a 1-year permit and '6×' for the 6-month type-D visa. WHY IT IS A BALANCE, NOT INCOME: MVR's migration page states the evidence vehicle as 'Документ от българска банка за наличие на средства за издръжка в размер не по-малък от...' (funds PRESENT in a Bulgarian bank), and Bulgarian practitioners state the point explicitly. legex.bg (https://legex.bg/en/resisdence-for-pensioners), verbatim-confirmed across two independently-worded fetches 2026-08-26: 'The amount of the pension is not regulated, but the amount in a Bulgarian bank account must be equal to 12 minimum pensions according to Bulgarian legislation.' The previously-cited bulgarianexpert.com (https://bulgarianexpert.com/rp/rp-retirees-en.html) independently gives the same structure — 'at least 6 Bulgarian minimum pensions' for the visa and 'at least twelve Bulgarian minimum pensions' for the permit — but on stale 2020 figures (250 lev), and it states no income requirement at all; it is no longer the source_url because it does not contain the current value. blacksealaw.com (https://www.blacksealaw.com/bg/service/razreshenie-za-prebivavane-na-chuzhdenci---pensioneri) corroborates the same statutory formula in Bulgarian. THE MINIMUM-PENSION FIGURE (primary): Bulgaria's Ministry of Labour and Social Policy (https://www.mlsp.government.bg/ministr-efremova-s-promeni-v-udlzhitelniya-zakon-za-byudzheta-garantirame-che-ot-1-yuli-minimalnata-pensiya-za-stazh-i-vzra, published 2026-05-19): 'от 1 юли минималната пенсия за осигурителен стаж и възраст ще нарасне от 322,37 евро на 347,51 евро' (+7.8%, Swiss-rule indexation). So 12× = €4,170.12 today; it was €3,868.44 (12 × €322.37) between 1 Jan and 30 Jun 2026, and re-indexes every 1 July. RESIDUAL UNCERTAINTY, NOT RESOLVED — WHICH BENCHMARK BINDS: the statutory clause is disjunctive (minimum wage OR minimum stipend OR minimum pension) and neither the MFA page, the iisda service record, nor the MVR page assigns one benchmark to the pensioner ground in its own words. Every practitioner source reads it as the minimum PENSION for this ground, which is why €4,170.12 is recorded. A stricter reading against the 2026 minimum WAGE (€620.20/month, ПМС № 243 of 13 Nov 2025) would give 12 × €620.20 = €7,442.40 — ~78% higher. Confidence stays Medium for exactly this reason: the mechanism and its shape are primary-sourced and certain; the multiplicand is practitioner-consensus, not primary-assigned. Anyone planning against this should budget for the wage-benchmark reading. DIVERGENCE (Confirmed-diverges): the statute's own words are flow-shaped — 'стабилни, редовни, предвидими и достатъчни средства за издръжка' (stable, regular, foreseeable and sufficient means) — while administrative practice tests a stock and expressly does not regulate the pension's size. A retiree with a large, regular pension and no Bulgarian deposit satisfies the statute's wording and fails the counter; a retiree with a small pension and €4,170.12 parked in a Bulgarian account passes. SUPERSEDED FIGURES PRESERVED: the 2026-07-11 original said '€6,000/year (~€500/month)', which conflated the minimum WAGE with the minimum PENSION (that diagnosis, from 2026-08-25, was correct and is unchanged).
