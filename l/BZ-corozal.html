  • General (non-QRP) Permanent Residency — residency/stability requirement

  • Pet import (cat) — species-specific requirement verification
    Materially the same regime as dogs, confirmed rather than assumed: rabies vaccination required ≥30 days before entry for cats over 3 months old (not recognized if administered >12 months prior); rabies titer test NOT required — veterinary exemption letters are not a substitute for vaccination proof; no mandatory quarantine
    pettravel.com, expatfocus.com, aphis.usda.gov, grandbaymen.com, 2026 — species-specific difference from dogs, checked directly rather than assumed per this project's own standing rule: cats additionally require vaccination against feline viral rhinotracheitis, calicivirus, and panleukopenia (vs. dogs' distemper/hepatitis/parvovirus/leptospirosis); international veterinary certificate must be issued within 14 days of entry; microchip recommended, not legally mandated for either species. Closes a real GAP row — the underlying regimes are materially similar, not identical, between the two species
  • Pet import (dog) — rabies titer / quarantine requirement
    No mandatory quarantine for pets meeting requirements; rabies titer test NOT required from any country — only recommended (not mandated) for pets originating in high-rabies-risk countries
    pettravel.com, aphis.usda.gov, grandbaymen.com, 2026 — rabies vaccination required at least 30 days before entry (multi-year vaccines not recognized unless administered within the past 12 months); also required: distemper/hepatitis/parvovirus/leptospirosis vaccination, internal/external parasite treatment within 30 days of entry by a licensed vet, export health certificate, import permit; ISO-compliant microchip recommended, not mandated. Pets under four months old (too young for the rabies vaccine) held in home quarantine until 30 days after vaccination becomes possible.
