Visa & residency

How you'd actually get to stay — the real routes, their income floors, and how long they realistically take, not the marketing version.

  • Border-zone property restriction for foreigners
    ~100km border-proximity scrutiny zone (mechanics read less bright-line than Mexico's)
    Source's own recommendation is to avoid these zones altogether, not merely get legal confirmation: "The most common workaround is simply avoiding these sensitive areas altogether and buying in mainstream urban locations like El Poblado in Medellin, Chapinero in Bogota, or Bocagrande in Cartagena, where no special authorization is needed." Restriction is location-based, not nationality-based, per the same source.
  • Digital Nomad Visa converts to permanent residency
    No
    Visitor(V)-category (Resolución 5477 de 2022) — time on it does not count toward the 5-year Migrant residency clock. Divergence flag closed 2026-07-08: the rule is stated unambiguously in statutory text, but no lived applicant account confirming this in practice was found; applies identically to Rentista
  • Digital Nomad Visa income-type requirement (passive)
    explicitly_rejected
    Foreign active income only — remote employees, freelancers/contractors with foreign clients, or digital entrepreneurs; explicitly excludes Colombian-sourced income; nothing found suggests passive/investment income qualifies.
  • Digital Nomad Visa income-type requirement (pension)
    explicitly_rejected
    Same closed-category-by-definition mechanism as this route's existing passive-income rejection: the qualifying-income definition (Resolución 5477 de 2022) is exhaustive and active-income-only — remote employee, freelancer/contractor, digital entrepreneur, content creator, or foreign-company shareholder/partner managing the business from Colombia (this last detail itself a bonus find this pass, via a search synthesis of expatgroup.co/gestionlegalcolombia.com). Pension income is not one of the named categories in any of 5+ sources checked. No source states an explicit textual pension exclusion — the primary regulation text remains unreachable (cancilleria.gov.co 403; icbf/suin-juriscol mirrors either blocked or non-resolution content this pass) — this is an inferred rejection by exhaustive-definition, the same evidentiary shape as this map's own MY DE Rantau and AR Pensionado precedent, not a primary-text-confirmed exclusion.
  • Digital Nomad Visa mandatory private "all-risk" health insurance, incl. repatriation
    Yes
    Ordinary public health-insurance (EPS) enrollment does not satisfy this visa requirement — a private/international policy is required, tightened 2026-07-09 to explicitly require medical-repatriation coverage (ordinary travel insurance no longer accepted). A genuine compound gate against the age-cutoff row above: someone past a local insurer's cutoff needs an international/travel product with a higher or no age cap to keep this visa route open. Not yet checked whether Pensionado carries the same explicit mandate
  • Digital Nomad Visa rejection-rate (reported tightening)
    20–30 % (reported rejection-rate increase; alt. stat: 58% approval rate, 2,918 applications rejected in 2025)
    Nearly every source repeating this traces back to a handful of trade-press/SEO-aggregator articles (travelandtourworld.com's own headline literally reads "Explosive Digital Nomad Visa Crisis"); the one quantified figure traces to Medellín Guru's own 2025 report (credible, but not a government statistic). Real, reported administrative tightening that could not independently verify against a primary government data source
  • Digital Nomad Visa (Visa V) income threshold
    1705–1715 $/month
    The threshold is set in pesos, not dollars: three times the 2026 monthly minimum wage (SMMLV), COP 5,252,715/month. The wage figure itself is contested — Decreto 1469 de 2025, which fixed it, was provisionally suspended by the Consejo de Estado in February 2026, and Decreto 0159 de 2026 re-fixed the same amount transitorily pending a final ruling. The USD band shown is our own conversion at the 2026-08-26 exchange rate (official TRM 3,081.67 COP/USD, resting on secondary reporting because the central bank's own site could not be reached; market mid 3,062.96 COP/USD) — no source publishes a current-rate dollar figure for this route, so the band is an estimate and will drift as the peso moves. Corrected 2026-08-26: the ~$1,400 previously shown here came from a stale, weaker-peso conversion (~3,750 COP/USD) and understated the bar by roughly 18%. Confirmed against the source's own text: there is no income averaging — each of the last three to six months' bank statements must individually clear the threshold. The peso's 2026 appreciation is raising the dollar-equivalent bar for dollar earners, and time on this V-category visa does not count toward the five-year residency clock.
