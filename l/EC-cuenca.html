  • Five-year foreign-income tax exemption for qualifying new residents
    Conditional — requires EITHER a USD 150,000 qualifying investment OR Ecuador-source income above USD 2,500/month with social-security contributions, plus SRI registration under this specific regime; runs 5 years from registration, not from arrival
    Thinnest-3 backfill (income-viability), 2026-07-18. This is the specific, real mechanism behind the '0% foreign income tax for five years' framing that circulates in expat-relocation marketing — genuinely real, but structurally narrower than a blanket new-resident perk: someone whose only income is foreign passive income (rental, dividends, a pension) and who is NOT making a $150k Ecuador investment does not automatically qualify just by becoming a resident — they would need Ecuador-source income over $2,500/month instead, which many passive-income-only profiles won't have. During the qualifying window, all foreign-source income (dividends, interest, capital gains, royalties, business profits) is exempt; after 5 years, standard worldwide taxation resumes if the 183-day trigger is met. Not independently verified against SRI's own primary text this pass (PDF unreadable, see the primary-source-attempt row).

Red flags

The hard truths, stated plainly — real risks, sitting right next to everything that's actually going well.

  • Cannabis law
    Recreational personal use illegal since Executive Decree No. 28 (Nov 24 2023); medical legal since a Sept 2019 National Assembly vote (83-23)
    Source-link backfill pass 2026-07-18 refinement: the repealing instrument has a real name and date — Executive Decree No. 28, issued by the Ministry of Interior within a day of Noboa taking office, repealing the prior 10g personal-use threshold. Despite the repeal, Ecuador's own criminal code (COIP) Articles 220/228 still state personal-use possession is not punishable, and the National Court of Justice's Resolution 14-2023 expressly confirmed those non-punishability rules remain 'fully in force' — quantity alone doesn't determine the crime, trafficking intent does. This is a more precise 'legal gray area' than the original pass's general framing, not a reversal of the worsening trajectory. Trafficking carries a 10-year minimum sentence.
  • Compound gate — visa-application insurance proof required before cédula/IESS eligibility exists
    Yes
    Rentista/Pensionado/Digital Nomad visas require proof of insurance at application, before a cédula exists — an older first-time applicant can hit a private-insurer age ceiling exactly when coverage is needed to file, with no IESS bridge yet available (myecuadorvisa.com, 2026)
  • Executive-judicial tension
    Yes
    Sustained friction with the Constitutional Court, plus fears of rights "regression" tied to the states-of-exception tool used against organized crime
  • IESS coverage scope
    Full network coverage, no copays/deductibles; pre-existing conditions covered after a 3-month wait; dental + prescriptions included
    cuencaexpat.com/myecuadorvisa.com, 2026
  • IESS voluntary affiliation — foreigner eligibility gate
    Requires legal residency (cédula); tourist-visa holders cannot enroll
    Private insurance is the only route pre-residency; distinct mechanism from the income-threshold visa gates above
  • IESS voluntary affiliation monthly cost
    84.83–264 $/month
    Minimum = 17.6% of the $482 SBU base (2026), rises if IESS bases the contribution on a higher declared visa income. Source-link backfill pass 2026-07-18, a real trajectory finding: IESS overhauled its voluntary-affiliate contribution formula in 2026, moving from a flat minimum-wage-pegged rate toward one based on the affiliate's own declared income — expats who budgeted around the old ~$85/month minimum are reportedly seeing bills 'nearly double' (cuencaexpat.com, 2026). The stated $84.83-264/month band still holds as the range, but the direction is now explicitly upward, not stable. A spousal add-on costs roughly 3.41% of the base (~$16.44/month at the 2026 minimum), making a couple's IESS floor ~$101.27/month.
  • Kidnapping/cartel-extortion risk specifically targeting foreigners (national)
    Express kidnapping and 'vacunas' (protection-money extortion) disproportionately affect tourists/foreign organizations per OSAC-sourced advisory material, concentrated in Guayaquil and coastal trafficking corridors; national extortion cases overall rose from 1,616 (2019) to 23,080 (2024) before a reported 30% drop to 16,131 (2025), though the drop is contested as under-reporting rather than real decline
    Distinct mechanism from the homicide-rate/armed-conflict finding above, now actually researched (closing the prior two passes' open gap). Express kidnapping (secuestro exprés): 2-6hr detention while attackers force ATM withdrawals, concentrated in evening hours in poorly-lit urban areas. Ransom demands for kidnapping-for-ransom range $10,000-$500,000 per multiple advisory sources. InsightCrime's own detailed extortion reporting names schools, transit/port workers, and businesses as the targeted population and does not mention foreigners/expats specifically — the foreigner-targeting pattern comes from separate OSAC-sourced general-advisory material, not the same underlying dataset, worth noting as a real distinction between sources rather than one unified picture.
  • National homicide rate
    51 per 100,000
    Among the highest in Latin America; government reported an 18% YoY reduction as of Nov 2024 off an even higher peak. Source-link backfill pass 2026-07-18 refinement, cross-corroborated by International Crisis Group: homicides surged 430% over the preceding five years, and the first half of 2025 was Ecuador's most violent semester on record — the 2024 dip and the 2025 surge are not contradictory (a temporary improvement followed by a fresh deterioration), but the fuller picture is more volatile than the original pass's single 18%-reduction figure implied on its own.
  • National state of internal armed conflict (declared Jan 2024)
    Yes
    Presidential Decree 111 designated 22 criminal groups as 'terrorist organizations' and authorized broad military deployment. Driven by transnational drug-trafficking organizations (Los Choneros, Los Lobos named as the two dominant groups) fighting for Pacific-coast smuggling-route control; military prison deployments since Jan 2024 incl. Cuenca's own facility, repeated states of exception. Source-link backfill pass 2026-07-18: cross-corroborated and refined via International Crisis Group's own 'Paradise Lost?' report — the declaration followed the prison escape of Los Choneros leader Adolfo Macías ('Fito') and an armed takeover of a live TV broadcast.
  • November 2025 constitutional referendum — rejected on all 4 questions
    60.75% No (foreign military bases); 58.20% No (end public party funding); 53.59% No (reduce Assembly seats); 61.71% No (new Constitution)
    President Noboa's first major electoral defeat; government described as "weakened" post-defeat though his party retains a stronger legislative position. Source-link backfill pass 2026-07-18: replaces the original pass's rounded '58-62% No' approximation with the precise per-question figures, cross-corroborated by colombiaone.com's independent reporting. Turnout exceeded 80% under Ecuador's mandatory-voting rule, per the National Electoral Council (CNE).
  • Pet import (cat) — species-specific requirement verification
    Same regime as dog, plus FVRCP
    **A genuine exception to this project's usual dog-only pet-import gap**: Andrés's pass explicitly researched both species together — cats need FVRCP in place of dogs' DHLPP + Bordetella, rabies titer (FAVN/RNATT) advised-not-required for pets originating from Africa/Asia/Oceania/Australia specifically
  • Pet import (dog) — rabies titer / quarantine requirement
    No quarantine with paperwork in order; Agrocalidad import permit ~$30, 1-2 weeks to process
    Rabies vaccination 21+ days before entry, health certificate within 10 days of travel, parasite treatment within 15 days; also needs DHLPP + Bordetella. Source-link backfill pass 2026-07-18: cross-corroborated by pettravel.com's own dedicated Ecuador pet-import page, plus a new detail found this pass — an Agrocalidad (Ecuador's own agricultural/animal-health agency) import permit is required, ~$30 online, processed in 1-2 weeks. A USDA Form 7001 health-certificate requirement cited by some sources is specifically a US-departure-point requirement, not universal — worth flagging per this project's nationality-neutral discipline rather than presenting it as a blanket rule.
  • Private health insurance — new-enrollment age ceiling
    Commonly cited 60–70; not reconciled to one figure age in years
    expatecuador.com cites 60–65, myecuadorvisa.com cites 65–70; a 2017 law reportedly bars age-based rejection of *existing* clients but whether/how that extends to new enrollment above the common ceiling is unresolved this pass, including this session's re-check.
  • WHO health-system ranking — corrected vs. a circulating expat-marketing claim
    111th of 211 countries (2000) improved to 20th (2014) rank
    en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Health_Organization_ranking_of_health_systems_in_2000, jabfm.org, ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/PMC, 2026.
