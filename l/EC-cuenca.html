  • Tax residents pay Ecuadorian tax on worldwide income, not just Ecuador-source income (shifted from a territorial system in 2021)
    Yes
    Effective since the Ley Orgánica para el Desarrollo Económico y Sostenibilidad Fiscal tras la Pandemia COVID-19 (Nov 2021). Thinnest-3 backfill (income-viability), 2026-07-18 — a real, load-bearing correction to a previously-open gap: `our records`'s original Income viability note flagged this as entirely unresearched, implicitly leaving open the possibility Ecuador worked like Paraguay's confirmed permanent territorial exemption. It does not. PwC's own tax summary states directly: 'Income generated abroad by Ecuador-resident individuals, local or foreign, is taxable.' A second source (sebsauerborn.com) independently confirms the 2021 law-change framing. A THIRD source found (countrytaxcalc.com) claims Ecuador runs 'a territorial tax system' with foreign income '100% exempt' — this appears to be an incorrect or dangerously oversimplified conflation of the temporary 5-year new-resident exemption (see the sibling fact row) with a permanent blanket rule; flagged here explicitly as a corrected/contradicted secondary claim, not silently dropped.
  • Five-year foreign-income tax exemption for qualifying new residents
    Conditional — requires EITHER a USD 150,000 qualifying investment OR Ecuador-source income above USD 2,500/month with social-security contributions, plus SRI registration under this specific regime; runs 5 years from registration, not from arrival
    Thinnest-3 backfill (income-viability), 2026-07-18. This is the specific, real mechanism behind the '0% foreign income tax for five years' framing that circulates in expat-relocation marketing — genuinely real, but structurally narrower than a blanket new-resident perk: someone whose only income is foreign passive income (rental, dividends, a pension) and who is NOT making a $150k Ecuador investment does not automatically qualify just by becoming a resident — they would need Ecuador-source income over $2,500/month instead, which many passive-income-only profiles won't have. During the qualifying window, all foreign-source income (dividends, interest, capital gains, royalties, business profits) is exempt; after 5 years, standard worldwide taxation resumes if the 183-day trigger is met. Not independently verified against SRI's own primary text (PDF unreadable, see the primary-source-attempt row).
  • Standard progressive personal income tax brackets, 2026
    0%–37% marginal rate, 9 brackets
    Thinnest-3 backfill (income-viability), 2026-07-18. Applies to a tax resident's total taxable income (worldwide, per the sibling fact row above) once any 5-year exemption window has ended or was never claimed.
