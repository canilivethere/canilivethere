    Yes (confirmed absent)

  • Overstay penalty
    14-day grace period, then ~1,685 EGP (+ admin fees) for first 3 months, escalating
    A relatively modest penalty structure compared to Belize's BZ$5,000/2-years-imprisonment statutory ceiling; exact escalation beyond 3 months not itemized. No source_url added 2026-07-18 — touristvisaonline.com's original citing page not re-verified to an exact URL this pass
  • Proposed 5-Year Multiple-Entry Visa
    Announced, NOT yet operational
    Reported $700 cost, ~180 countries eligible; no application process, eligibility detail, or effective date published. No source_url added 2026-07-18 — citizenremote.com's original citing page not re-verified to an exact URL this pass
  • Real-Estate-Investment Residency — leasehold eligibility
    Not yet researched
    Whether a 99-year Sinai leasehold (the only instrument foreigners can hold in Dahab) qualifies as the "investment" this program requires, or whether it requires freehold title specifically (unavailable in Dahab), every source describing the program cites documentation language that reads as assuming freehold ownership. Still the top unresolved question as of 2026-07-18 — not closed this pass
  • Real-Estate-Investment Residency — tiered thresholds
    $50,000 → 1yr; $100,000 → 3yr; $200,000 → 5yr USD invested
    A genuinely operational program, renewable while ownership is maintained; does not require personally occupying the unit (can be rented). Extends to spouse and children. Cross-corroborated 2026-07-18 against thehorizonrealestate.com's own description of the same three tiers
  • Real-Estate-Investment Residency converts to permanent residency
    No
    A renewable temporary-residence mechanism tied to continued ownership, not a direct route to full PR
  • Taxes on foreign/remote income
    183-day/year physical-presence tax-residency test; residents taxed 0-22.5% progressive on worldwide income
    Filled this pass — previously a named [GAP]. Not independently verified against Egypt's own Income Tax Law text, a real, named gap consistent with this file's other unchecked-primary-source items. Below the 183-day threshold, no Egyptian tax on foreign-sourced income; crossing it is a real, structural exposure directly relevant to a genuinely long-stay (3-year-plus) relocation intent, distinct from Dahab's low cost-of-living reputation
  • Remote work formally recognized in Egyptian labor law
    Yes
    New this pass — Labor Law No. 14 of 2025 formally recognizes remote work as a legitimate, regulated employment arrangement in Egyptian law for the first time. Does not create a new visa category — regulates employment-law status for people already present, not entry/residency itself. A real, dated legal-clarity improvement, not a static "grey area" the Income viability criterion might otherwise default to describing
  • Freelancer Tax Registration Number (TRN) threshold
    60000 EGP/year (≈$1,150-1,200 USD)
    New this pass. Presented by the secondary source as applying to freelancers generally, filing annual returns by March 31 — whether and how it applies specifically to a foreign resident earning entirely foreign-sourced income while under the 183-day tax-residency threshold was NOT resolved this pass, hence divergence_flag = Not yet checked rather than assumed to apply
  • Double-taxation treaty network
    50+ countries (including US, UK)
    New this pass. Relevant if both the 183-day Egyptian tax-residency threshold is crossed and a home-country tax obligation also exists — mitigates but does not eliminate the need to check both sides
  • Tourist e-visa / visa-on-arrival stay allowance
    30 days/visit
    Single-entry e-visa valid 90 days for use (stay still capped at 30 days); multi-entry e-visa valid 180 days. Visa-on-arrival available to ~45-50 nationalities for $25-30 USD cash. Upgraded 2026-07-18: source_url is Egypt's own official e-visa portal, a primary institutional source, directly confirming the 30-day figure — the single largest visa-mechanics gap this file previously named is now closed. A separate, narrower visa-free allowance (under 15 days, air arrival into Sharm El-Sheikh specifically, resorts only) also exists per gov.uk, distinct from this general route
  • Tourist visa extension
    Additional 30 days available in Egypt; discretionary range of 30-90 days reported depending on circumstances
    Fee ~$20-30. Processed at Abbassia, Cairo, or (closer to Dahab) Alexandria/Hurghada/Sharm El Sheikh/Luxor. The discretion-based range is itself real uncertainty, not a firm number. No source_url added 2026-07-18 — kadmartravel.com/evisa-egypt.info not re-verified to exact URLs this pass
  • Work permit / employment-based residency — precondition-gated
    Requires an Egyptian employer sponsor
    Not researched to full mechanical depth this pass. Still open as of 2026-07-18

Red flags

The hard truths, stated plainly — real risks, sitting right next to everything that's actually going well.

  • Cannabis law
    Illegal; personal-use possession min. 1yr imprisonment + fines from 1,000 EGP; cultivation w/ trafficking intent up to death penalty
    Anti-Narcotics Law No. 182/1960 (amended 1989) — the harshest cannabis regime found in this project to date. No leniency for foreign nationals; CBD also illegal. A structural, hard-blocker-tier risk. Upgraded 2026-07-18: source_url is UNODC's own hosted copy of Law No. 122 of 1989's actual statutory text (also mirrored in HTML at unodc.org/cld/legislation/egy/...), a genuine primary-institutional source, not a law-firm paraphrase — source_count upgraded from single to primary-institutional accordingly
  • External debt / fiscal fragility
    $27-29B debt service due 2026, vs. current-account deficit narrowed to ~4.2% of GDP USD
    Interest payments now exceed half of total government expenditure — a real structural fiscal-fragility backdrop, even though the currency trajectory is improving. Upgraded 2026-07-18: source_url is IMF Country Report No. 26/69 (Arab Republic of Egypt), the actual primary document — the Central Bank of Egypt's own mid-2026 revision raised its debt-service estimate from $27.87B to $29.18B, both figures now presented as a range rather than the original pass's single $27B figure
  • North Sinai insurgency
    Yes (active, ongoing)
    UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) "do not travel" advisory for North Sinai; ISIS-Sinai Province insurgency running over a decade. Attacks on security forces "still occur but are becoming increasingly rare" per one 2026 source — real but geographically distinct from Dahab's own South Sinai coastal zone. Cross-corroboration upgraded 2026-07-18: the US State Department's own, independently-issued Egypt Travel Advisory (travel.state.gov) confirms the same North/South Sinai split — two governments' own primary text, not one source paraphrased twice
  • Pet import (cat) — species-specific requirement
    Same rabies/microchip timing as dog, but vaccination panel is feline viral rhinotracheitis/calicivirus/panleukopenia instead
    A sixth genuine exception to this project's dog-only pet-import gap. No specific quarantine requirement found for either species — treat as an open question, not confirmed "no quarantine" the way Belize's research explicitly confirmed. Not re-researched 2026-07-18
  • Pet import (dog) — vaccination requirement
    Rabies vaccine 30 days–11 months before arrival, no titer required; also distemper/hepatitis/parvovirus/parainfluenza
    ISO microchip mandatory, must be inserted BEFORE rabies vaccination (specific sequencing requirement). Not re-researched 2026-07-18
  • Political leadership stability
    President Sisi's government reads as stable/continuous authoritarian-leaning system, no named 2026 transition event found
    Not independently verified against a dedicated political-stability source — a real gap relative to Belize's/Cape Town's more thorough treatment. Still unresearched as of 2026-07-18
  • South Sinai inland-interior advisory
    'All but essential travel' advisory beyond the St Catherine-Nuweibaa road; does NOT cover the coastal areas/Dahab itself
    An inland-desert-interior warning — any off-route inland excursion (desert safaris, Mount Sinai treks) needs checking against this boundary separately from Dahab's own coastal "low risk" status
  • 2006 Dahab bombings

    Three bombs in tourist areas (Nelson restaurant, Aladdin café, Ghazala market); part of a documented mid-2000s pattern of Sinai tourist-town bombings (2004 Taba, 2005 Sharm El-Sheikh, both separate towns). No source_url added 2026-07-18 pass — original citing domains (pureniletours.com, egypttravelways.com) not re-verified to an exact page this pass, an honest gap rather than a guessed link
