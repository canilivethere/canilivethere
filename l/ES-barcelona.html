    2800 people (march)
  • Anti-tourism protest — June 2025 (coordinated cross-city)

  • First-hand account (David William Rosales, digital nomad)
    David William Rosales: U.S.-based digital nomad, visited Barcelona 6+ times with 1+ month work-remote stays across 4 visits. Describes Barcelona as 'a second home.' Key findings: Housing expensive (short-term rentals pricey due to STR restrictions), but food 'reasonable' at both grocery and restaurant level (menú lunches €12-16). Infrastructure strong for remote work—reliable fast wifi, abundant coworking spaces (toured multiple, chose OneCoWork €220/mo for 'intimate' atmosphere). Cultural integration deliberate: speaks Spanish/Catalan, emphasizes language basics for genuine local connections. Appreciates city's 'combination of slow and fast paced' culture balancing productive work with lifestyle.
  • Rent growth (decade)
    ~70 % (past decade)
  • Room for others / group viability
    The active, physically-confrontational backlash makes a visible group arrival a real, live risk-shape question
Red flags

The hard truths, stated plainly — real risks, sitting right next to everything that's actually going well.

  • Alcohol law/culture
    Legal drinking age 18; ordinary EU-standard Mediterranean alcohol culture — wine/beer normal everyday presence, not restricted
  • Cannabis law
    Decriminalized for private use in a private residence (~100g commonly cited); public consumption/sale/trafficking illegal, fines €601-€30,000 per Ley Orgánica 4/2015
  • Catalan independence question
    Underlying constitutional-status question remains unresolved but has receded from the 2017 acute-crisis level
  • Eurozone/EU membership — currency-crisis risk removal
    Yes
  • Minority coalition government (PSOE/Sumar + regional parties)
    Junts withdrew backing fall 2025 over deportation-powers dispute, itself pressured by rise of Catalan far-right party Aliança Catalana
  • Pet import (dog and cat) — rabies titer / quarantine requirement
    No divergence found between species; microchip THEN rabies vaccine 21+ days before travel; NO titer required for US-origin pets; NO quarantine with complete documentation
  • Pet-travel health-certificate format change
    New EU non-commercial pet-travel health certificates take effect Oct 1, 2026 (commercial certs Oct 17, 2026)
  • Illegal short-term-rental listing removal (national enforcement)
    54000 listings removed nationally
  • Homicide rate — no real Barcelona-specific figure found; Spain's national rate shown as the only available reference point
    0.80 homicides per 100,000 inhabitants (Spain, national — NOT Barcelona-specific)
  • Numbeo Crime Index
    51.89–51.96 index score (Safety Index ~48.11)
  • Pickpocketing share of recorded crime
    48.1 % (2023)
  • Short-term-rental phase-out (municipal policy)
    All ~10,000 licensed STR units phased out by November 2028 — no renewals once expired
  • Resident tourism-capacity perception survey
    60 % of residents surveyed
  • Airbnb-driven resident population decline (select neighborhoods)
    0–45 % decline, 2007-2019, select neighborhoods
  • Natural hazard — 2026 wildfire season (Catalonia-wide, no direct hit on Barcelona city)
    150+ wildfires in Catalonia since start of summer 2026 (2nd-highest count at this point in the year since 2010); ~3,000ha burned Jun 1-Jul 5, 2026; closest named fire (Sentmenat, ~200ha) ~24km straight-line/37km road from Barcelona city, inside the Barcelona metro area
