Visa & residency

How you'd actually get to stay — the real routes, their income floors, and how long they realistically take, not the marketing version.

  • Compound / small-group co-ownership model
    comunidad de bienes (undivided joint ownership) + propiedad horizontal (Ley 49/1960) — same functional pattern as Guatemala/Colombia/Mexico/Argentina/Portugal
    Sixth confirmed country following this cross-candidate compound/co-ownership pattern. Not independently verified to primary Civil Code article numbers — named consistently across property-law sources
  • Convenio Especial (public-system buy-in) — eligibility and age-gated cost
    12mo residency + not employed/self-employed in Spain required; €60/mo under 65, €157/mo 65+ €/month
    Open-application window after 12mo legal residency under any valid visa (Digital Nomad, NLV, student, etc.). Covers primary care, specialist consultations, hospital, emergency services, surgeries. Excludes subsidized prescription-drug rates — the single biggest gap for chronic medication. Not free entitlement even after years of NLV residence. Cross-corroborated: MySpainVisa, MovingToSpain, Waypoint Sur, CostaPluswer, SpainVisaHealth. Official page (Ministerio de Sanidad) also available.
  • Digital Nomad Visa (DNV) income threshold
    2850 €/month (200% of SMI, ~€34,200/year)
    Effective under Royal Decree 126/2026. +€916/month first dependent, +€305/month per additional dependent. Threshold rises automatically whenever Spain's minimum wage rises — a structural difference from Portugal's D8, whose threshold only moves on separate Portuguese action Re-verified 2026-08-25 ([specialist]): jobbatical.com confirms the 200% of Spain's minimum wage (SMI) basis and dependent figures (+€916 first, +€305 additional) exactly; page states €2,849 vs this row's €2,850 -- trivial rounding, not a defect.
  • Digital Nomad Visa (DNV) accepts passive income as qualifying proof
    No
    Requires a university degree OR 3+ years documented professional experience, employment with sponsoring company 3+ months pre-application, Spanish-sourced income capped at 20% of total — active remote-employment income, not a fit for a passive-income profile
  • Digital Nomad Visa (DNV) duration
    Up to 1yr if applied from abroad via consulate; up to 3yr if applied from inside Spain
    A genuinely different (better) shape than most guides' default description of the shorter consulate route
  • Entry/Exit System (EES) operational status
    Yes
    Fully operational across all Schengen countries as of April 10, 2026 (progressive rollout began Oct 2025). Manual stamping replaced with biometric digital records (facial images, fingerprints, passport data). Closed the practical enforcement gap that previously made border-hopping quietly tolerated. 45+ million border crossings registered by April 2026; 24,000 refused entry. All 29 Schengen countries deployed at external borders.
  • Employer-sponsored work visa / EU Blue Card
    Not yet researched
    Requires a job offer from a Spanish-registered employer; EU Blue Card requires a salary threshold tied to Spain's average gross salary
  • Entrepreneur Visa (Emprendedor) — precondition-gated
    No minimum investment; gated on a business plan judged 'innovative'/'special economic interest,' favorable report required from Spain's foreign-investment promotion agency (ICEX)-Invest in Spain
    Governed by Article 70 of Law 14/2013, survived the 2025 Golden Visa reform. Initial residence authorization 3 years, renewable. Requires an actual operating business — not a fit for passive capital or buy-to-let, the same hands-on-not-passive-capital shape as Portugal's D2. 2026-08-24 citation-integrity recheck ([specialist]): this comparative framing is this project's own analytical label (also used to compare against Portugal's D2 visa), never presented as a quote from vissumlex.com -- confirmed directly via live fetch that the exact phrase does not appear on that page (closest genuine text there: "Legalizing resident status now requires active economic participation through the creation or funding of innovative commercial structures" and "The state vector has shifted from the real estate market to the high-tech sector and venture financing").   • Family reunification (non-EU sponsor)

    Under RD 1155/2024, spouses/registered partners/children 16+ get automatic work authorization alongside the reunification permit — a real, named improvement over the prior separate-work-permit requirement
  • Golden Visa

    Confirmed via Spain's own government transparency portal (one.gob.es). Applications filed before the cutoff still processed; existing holders can renew under original criteria.     Yes

  • Private health-insurance new-enrollment age ceiling
