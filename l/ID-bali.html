Red flags

The hard truths, stated plainly — real risks, sitting right next to everything that's actually going well.

  • Alcohol law/culture
    Legal drinking age 21 nationally, applied uniformly regardless of home-country age; majority-Muslim nation, more restricted/taxed in many regions
  • Bali-specific pet-import routing constraint
    Non-rabies-free-origin pets (incl. US) must enter via a mainland port (most commonly Jakarta Soekarno-Hatta), undergo quarantine there, then transport onward to Bali
  • Cannabis law
    Group I narcotic (Law 35/2009); personal use up to 4yr; possession up to 12yr + fines to 8B rupiah (~$560k); trafficking 1kg+/5+ plants: death penalty, life, or 5-20yr
  • Pet import (dog and cat) — rabies titer / quarantine requirement
    Microchip then rabies vaccine 30 days-1yr before export; a rabies-antibody titer test (FAVN) required from most origins (≥0.5 IU/ml); import permit required in advance; 7-14 day quarantine
  • Political stability vs. investment-climate signals
    Prabowo positions Indonesia as a 'calm investment bet' (stability, fiscal discipline); military/police moving into senior political roles; rupiah became Asia's worst-performing currency in 2026, Jakarta Composite Index at lowest since May 2021
  • "Sex outside marriage" criminal provision (2026 Criminal Code)
    Up to 1yr imprisonment (sex outside marriage), up to 6mo (unmarried cohabitation); applies to foreigners on Indonesian territory
  • Rupiah currency crisis (2026)
    18064–18155 IDR/USD (historic low, June 2026)
  • #IndonesiaGelap ("Dark Indonesia") protest movement
    Nationwide protest movement launched 2025-02-17 (BEM SI-organized), involving students, labor unions, teacher unions, online motorcycle-taxi drivers, and civil-society/church groups; demands include repealing 2025 budget-cut Presidential Instruction No.1, rejecting Armed Forces Law revisions that expand military roles in civilian government, transparency in the Free Nutritious Meals program, and an asset-confiscation bill; continued into 2026 with student protests on 2026-06-12 and 2026-06-15 specifically over fuel-price hikes, currency weakening, and government spending
  • Cabinet reshuffle frequency under Prabowo
    3 documented cabinet reorganizations in ~2 years (Feb 2025; two in Sept 2025)
  • Cybercrime/financial fraud trend
    Yes
  • Distance to emergency care
    40-70 minutes drive time in evening traffic
  • Hiking-access closure (religious ceremony, not safety)
    Closed to all hiking until April 24, 2026 for Karya Ida Bhatara Turun Kabeh ceremonies at Besakih Temple; reopened April 25, 2026
  • Kidnapping/cartel-extortion risk specifically targeting foreigners
    Limited/ad-hoc, not systematic cartel activity: 26 foreigners held in scam compound (April 2026), isolated murder/stabbing cases reported; police data shows foreign-crime cases DOWN 23% Jan-Apr 2026 vs same period 2025; no organized cartel-extortion pattern found
  • Medical evacuation for serious cases
    2 hours by air; AUD 30,000-50,000 for an air ambulance
  • Numbeo safety index
    48.97 (Safety Index); Crime Index 51.03 index score
  • Petty theft / phone-snatching
    Yes
  • Seismic and tsunami risk
    Active seismic setting (Ring of Fire); recent activity M6.0 Bali Sea (Sept 2025) and M5.3 Bali/East Java plate (Sept 2025), M7.1 regional (Aug 2023, Lombok); a BMKG-modeled M8.5 megathrust scenario could produce a 6-10m tsunami reaching Serangan/Sanur in ~27 minutes; siren warning systems installed at Kuta, Sanur, Nusa Dua, Tanjung Benoa, Kedonganan, and parts of Seminyak, though coverage is acknowledged as sparse (one 2026 official figure: 9 sirens for 153 high-risk villages island-wide) and absent at Canggu/Legian/Uluwatu; same-site tsunami recurrence interval 30-300 years (vs. ~2 years for a destructive tsunami somewhere in Indonesia generally)
  • Traffic accidents
    Yes
  • Volcanic risk
    Alert Level 1 — Normal (lowest of 4-level scale); last eruption June 13, 2019; unrest (vapor/smoke) reported through late 2025
  • Uluwatu/Bukit traffic congestion — government engineering response
    Chronic congestion in the Pecatu-Uluwatu tourism corridor; a traffic-flow restriction system (turn restrictions across 6 intersections, enforced 5-10pm daily) trialed June 2026 was made permanent by Badung Regency's Transportation Agency as of July 2026
  • Overtourism / visitor-volume strain — multi-year trajectory
    Not a new 2026 phenomenon: a 2017 "garbage emergency" occurred during a ~5.7-million-visitor year; Bali was already planning a US$10 tourist tax as of 2020; 2026 saw an active government beach-cleanup crisis response — a real, multi-year worsening pattern, not a one-off event
