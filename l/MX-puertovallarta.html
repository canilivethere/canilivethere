Red flags

The hard truths, stated plainly — real risks, sitting right next to everything that's actually going well.

  • Judicial Reform Phase 2 (direct judicial elections)
    Yes
  • USMCA 2026 review outcome
    Trilateral joint review held July 1, 2026 — no consensus to automatically extend the agreement for a further 16 years; US opted for annual reviews instead, citing trade-deficit concerns
  • Peso currency stability, explicitly tied to USMCA review risk
    Traded in a narrow 17-18 MXN/USD band through 2026 so far, backed by over USD 257 billion in international reserves; risk framed as binary and directly tied to the USMCA outcome — an acrimonious breakdown could push the pair toward 20+
  • Political continuity — Sheinbaum administration
    Sheinbaum (Morena) won a strong 2024 mandate; no near-term election, coup, or leadership-crisis risk — next presidential election is 2030
  • Foreign direct investment (FDI) resilience despite USMCA uncertainty
    23.6 $ billion, Q1 2026 record US FDI
  • CJNG succession conflict
    unsettled
  • Jalisco safety advisory tier
    US State Dept Level 3
  • CJNG's Mendoza Gaytan ("El Sapo") named national second-in-command, not merely a city plaza boss
    Hugo Gonzalo Mendoza Gaytan ("El Sapo") named by US authorities as CJNG's national second-in-command (tied with since-captured Audias Flores Silva, "El Jardinero") under new top boss Juan Carlos Valencia Gonzalez ("El Pelon"/"El 03"), following El Mencho's Feb 2026 death -- a materially bigger claim than a single city's plaza boss. Separately, he has led CJNG's "Los Deltas"/"Los Michoacanos" armed cell covering the Jalisco/Nayarit coast (Puerto Vallarta, Nuevo Vallarta, Bahia de Banderas, Sierra Occidente, Valle de Jalisco) since 2017 -- Feb 2026 reporting (pre-dating the national-rank news) named him CJNG's Puerto Vallarta-specific regional chief. Carries a $15 million US reward (DOJ/State Dept, announced 2026-08-05) and is reported -- per Mexico News Daily citing US court filings, not independently primary-source-verified -- to be a godson of El Mencho.
