How you'd actually get to stay — the real routes, their income floors, and how long they realistically take, not the marketing version.

  • Cross-route PR-conversion summary
    None of Thailand's four routes offers a clean, income-level-consistent path from legally-resident to PR
    The Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) and Privilege don't clear the first hurdle at all; Non-Immigrant O-A/O-X and the Long-Term Resident (LTR) visa (on one of two conflicting readings) clear the 3-year-clock precondition, but neither route's own qualifying income/asset test satisfies any of the five substantive PR categories on its own. No single official Thai Immigration Bureau page for PR mechanics exists — search results are dominated by immigration-law-firm sites, not a ตม./Immigration Bureau page of its own. siam-legal.com is the strongest available secondary source.
