    Fall leaf season (esp. 2nd-3rd weeks of October) is the single busiest stretch of the year — Blue Ridge Parkway traffic backups on peak weekends, downtown parking/restaurant demand spikes, hotels and short-term rentals booked out months in advance at spiked rates; summer separately shows a documented 29.8% spike in visitor volume over baseline.
