  • Tourist-season crowding — summer peak, fall festival growth
    Summer carries ~34% of annual visitors and spring ~33%, with June/July/March running about twice as busy as the quietest winter months — a genuine winter off-season, unlike Asheville. Fall has grown sharply as its own draw: Hamilton County's visitor economy hit a record $1.8 billion in 2024 (+4% over 2023's ~$1.7B), with downtown bracing for 'hundreds of thousands' of fall-2025 visitors anchored by the inaugural World of Bluegrass festival plus 150+ area fall festivals.
  • EPB gigabit fiber coverage and measured statewide speed
    EPB (city-owned electric utility) has run citywide gigabit fiber since 2010, escalating to 10 Gig (2015), 25 Gig (2022, aimed at businesses/remote workers), and a new 5 Gig symmetrical tier (July 2026). Chattanooga-area fiber coverage runs ~93% per provider-coverage aggregators. Ookla's own measured data puts Tennessee's statewide average download speed at 308 Mbps / 95 Mbps upload — a real, state-level (not Chattanooga-specific) measured figure, distinct from EPB's advertised tier ceilings.
