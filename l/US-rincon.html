    Domes/Tres Palmas world-class surf break, Punta Higüero whale-watching (winter)
  • Seasonal population/crowding swing — winter surf season vs. summer-through-fall low season
    Winter swells (roughly December-April) draw surfers and spring-break crowds to the Domes/Tres Palmas breaks, with December-February bringing the biggest waves, peak whale-watching, and the year's highest accommodation demand. April-October is the genuine low season — long flat-surf spells, markedly fewer visitors, lower prices. This quiet/cheap/uncrowded window overlaps almost exactly with Atlantic hurricane season (June-November) and the power grid's most exposed months — Rincón is least crowded exactly when it is most exposed to storm/grid risk, and busiest with visitors during its calmest weather.

  • Federal-vs-fiscal-oversight-board power struggle (2025)
    The federal administration attempted to dismiss three members of the fiscal oversight board (PROMESA) (Biggs, González, Rosa) — a body PROMESA itself designed to be independent of exactly this kind of executive interference. All three provisionally reinstated October 2025 after prevailing in a lawsuit arguing they'd been removed without the "just cause" PROMESA requires
  • Fiscal oversight board's own transparency failure
    As of January 2026, the board had gone a full year without publishing members' legally-required financial disclosures — roughly 50 missing filings, including 33 quarterly reports for Jan-Sept 2025 and 11 annual 2024 reports

  • Statehood-status question — genuinely stalled, not a live risk
    The Puerto Rico Status Act (which would have set a binding plebiscite for November 2, 2025) never passed Congress — passed the House once in 2022 in a different form, died in the Senate; reintroduced in the 118th Congress, never reached a floor vote in either chamber
