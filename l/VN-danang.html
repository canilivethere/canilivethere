    114500 USD, lump-sum registered-investment capital, roughly (VND 3,000,000,000 — the ĐT3-tier floor — converted at ~26,200 VND/USD, 2026-08-09)

CRITICAL finding for this specific route/fact: cross-corroborated across 5 independent sources that ĐT4 (the bottom tier, under ~$114,500) receives only a 12-month DT visa and is NOT eligible for a Temporary Residence Card at all — the TRC itself (this fact's own subject) only becomes available at ĐT3 and above, i.e. the real floor for 'TRC via Registered Investment' as a route is VND 3,000,000,000 (~$114,500 at the cited rate), not zero and not undocumented. Above that floor, ĐT3 grants a TRC up to 3 years (some sources say up to 10 years once combined with the visa's own renewal path — sources disagree on the exact TRC ceiling for ĐT3 specifically, see the companion tier-structure row), ĐT2/ĐT1 grant longer TRCs (5-10 years) for larger capital — those upper tiers affect duration, not whether the TRC route applies at all.   • Temporary Residence Card (TRC) via Registered Investment converts to permanent residency
    No
    RESOLVED 2026-08-22 ([specialist]; ask: [specialist], our records, sole remaining data_gap cause for marek/waldo x VN both grains). Read Article 39 of Law No. 47/2014/QH13 (Vietnam's governing entry/exit/transit/residence law) directly and verbatim, not via aggregator paraphrase: permanent residence is considered for exactly four statutory categories -- (1) foreigners awarded medals/titles by the Vietnamese government for contributions to Vietnam's development/protection, (2) foreign scientists/experts residing in Vietnam (requires a ministerial proposal per Article 40.2), (3) a foreigner sponsored by a parent/spouse/child who is a Vietnamese citizen with permanent residence (this category carries the sourced 3-consecutive-years-of-temporary-residence eligibility clock, Article 40.3 -- notably this clock applies ONLY to the family-sponsored category, not generically to any TRC holder), and (4) stateless persons with Vietnamese temporary residence dating to 2000 or earlier. Foreign investors (the DT visa/TRC category, any tier DT1-DT4) are not named in any of the four categories -- investor status is not merely a slower or conditional path to permanent residence (PR), it is genuinely excluded from the permanent-residence law entirely. An investor's TRC, however long its own validity runs (up to 10 years at the DT1 tier per the companion capital-tier-structure row), does not itself accrue toward or qualify for permanent residence. Primary-source access: thuvienphapluat.vn, vbpl.vn, and vanban.chinhphu.vn were all checked directly again this pass and found unreachable, same pattern as the 2026-08-11 threshold pass on this same route.
