Overview
  • Foreigner access to public healthcare system
    No — expats ineligible for subsidized public insurance; public facilities usable only at full non-subsidized cash rate
  • Private/international health insurance — annual premium range
    200–4547 $/year (individual)
  • Private/international health insurance — new-enrollment age ceiling
    60–76 years old (varies by insurer)
  • Alcohol culture
    Legal, cheap, deeply culturally normalized — bia hơi and regional beer identity (Huda in the central region)
  • Climate
    Tropical monsoon climate — annual avg ~26°C, hot season (Jun-Aug) 28-30°C, cool season (Dec-Feb) 18-23°C; annual rainfall ~2,205mm, concentrated Sep-Dec (550-1,000mm/month, typhoon-exposed); dry Jan-Aug (23-40mm/month Feb-Apr); humidity 81% annual avg (84-86% Oct-Jan, 75-77% Jun-Aug)
  • Typhoon-season routine disruption
    Sep-Dec wet season brings the heaviest monthly rainfall (550-1,000mm/month) and real typhoon exposure — a recurring seasonal disruption to outdoor routine (beach, scooter commuting) distinct from the dry Jan-Aug months
  • Distance to emergency care
    In-city — Vinmec International Hospital (JCI-accredited) + Hoan My Hospital, both central
  • Foreign-buyer neighborhood concentration
    Son Tra and Ngu Hanh Son districts (adjacent to An Thuong/My Khe beach)
  • Healthcare system capability — complex/specialist care ceiling
    Complex oncology, rare neurological disorders, major trauma, and complex surgery generally require medevac — Da Nang triages, doesn't terminally treat these cases
  • Healthcare system capability — routine/standard care
    Joint Commission International (JCI) Gold Seal-accredited private hospital (Vinmec) + Hoan My deliver Western-standard care across most specialties plus a real 24-hour ER
  • Internet speed (advertised residential)
    280–310 Mbps (~$7/month)
  • Medical evacuation cost and destinations
    15000–50000 $ (per evacuation, "+" for high end)
  • No first-hand multi-year foreign-resident account
    Not yet researched
  • Private hospitalization cost (per stay)
    1800 €+ (can exceed)
  • Private outpatient consultation cost
    15–126 €/visit (generalist–specialist)
  • Nature — My Khe Beach (China Beach)
    White sandy beach directly adjacent to the An Thuong nomad corridor (5-10 min walk from most My An properties); described as an "endless stretch"; resorts and surfing facilities present
  • Nature — Son Tra Peninsula
    Forested mountain peninsula, 60 km² district, wild streams and resorts along the seaside, sits directly adjacent to An Thuong/My Khe
  • Nature — Marble Mountains and Ba Na Hills
    Marble Mountains: limestone karst formations with the Huyen Khong Cave. Ba Na Hills: mountain resort, 5km cable car system, peak elevation 1,487m, cooler climate (~20°C annual average)
  • Nature/heritage — Hoi An and Hai Van Pass proximity
    Hoi An ancient town ~45-minute drive south; Hue accessible via a 2-hour scenic drive over the Hai Van Pass
  • Traffic Index and commute time
    103.19 (Traffic Index) / 27.38 (avg one-way commute, minutes) index points / minutes
  • Quality of Life Index (Numbeo composite)
    119.41 index points, rated "Low" by Numbeo's own scale
  • Qualitative routine account — My An vs. Hoa Xuan
    My An (An Thuong): "Where else can you finish your work call at 5 PM, and by 5:15 PM, be surfing, hiking up a mountain, or playing golf at a world-class course?"; "Extreme Convenience" (coworking, gyms, yoga studios, international grocers, vegan cafes within reach of the beach) but also "Between the endless construction, the traffic, and the nightlife on the infamous Ngo Thi Sy street, it can be relentlessly loud." Hoa Xuan: "It's quiet—sometimes, very quiet. The roads are wide, traffic is light, and the houses are spread out."
Visa & residency

How you'd actually get to stay — the real routes, their income floors, and how long they realistically take, not the marketing version.

  • Working without a required work permit — penalty
    15000000–25000000 VND fine + expulsion
  • Personal income tax — tax-residency threshold (183-day rule)

    5–35 % (progressive brackets, on worldwide income)

  • Temporary Residence Card (TRC) via Employment — structure
    Requires a Vietnamese work permit; a Temporary Residence Card (TRC) is then issued for 6 months to 5 years depending on underlying category
  • Temporary Residence Card (TRC) via Employment accepts passive income as qualifying proof
    No
  • Temporary Residence Card (TRC) via Marriage/Family — precondition-gated
    Real but conditional — requires a Vietnamese-citizen spouse/family member
  • Temporary Residence Card (TRC) via Registered Investment — capital threshold
    114500 USD, lump-sum registered-investment capital, roughly (VND 3,000,000,000 — the ĐT3-tier floor — converted at ~26,200 VND/USD, 2026-08-09)
  • Temporary Residence Card (TRC) via Registered Investment — full ĐT1-ĐT4 tier structure
    ĐT1 ≥VND100bn (~$3.8M): visa up to 5yr, Temporary Residence Card (TRC) up to 10yr. ĐT2 VND50-<100bn (~$1.9-3.8M): visa up to 5yr, TRC up to 5yr (one source: 10yr). ĐT3 VND3-<50bn (~$114.5k-1.9M): visa up to 3yr, TRC up to 2-3yr (one source: 10yr — sources disagree on ĐT3's TRC ceiling specifically). ĐT4 <VND3bn (~<$114.5k): visa only, up to 12 months, no TRC.

    No
  • Temporary Residence Card (TRC) via Registered Investment converts to permanent residency
    No

Red flags

The hard truths, stated plainly — real risks, sitting right next to everything that's actually going well.

  • 2026 leadership consolidation (Tô Lâm)
    General Secretary + President combined in one person for the first time in 4 decades; renewed 5-year mandate, retirement-age exemption
  • Cannabis law
    Illegal, death penalty for trafficking 5kg+ resin / 75kg+ leaves-stems-seeds (Art. 251); 2-7yr imprisonment for simple possession 1-500g (Art. 249)
  • Foreign investment posture
    A real, current draw — 2024 US FDI stock $4.4B, up 8% from 2023, driven by China-cost/tariff-driven supply-chain diversification
  • One-party political system (CPV)
    Durably controlled; genuine political stability expected precisely because dissent is suppressed and power is centralized
  • Pet import (dog and cat) — rabies titer / quarantine requirement
    Rabies vaccination 30 days–12 months before travel; 15-digit ISO microchip required; quarantine status genuinely conflicting across sources
  • Kidnapping/cartel-extortion risk specifically targeting foreigners
    No organized kidnapping/cartel targeting foreigners found; isolated May 2026 incident involved foreign nationals (Chinese) arrested for illegal detention and robbery; violent crime targeting foreigners extremely rare
  • Named scam patterns
    Fake Grab drivers at airport, fraudulent booking sites, taxi-meter manipulation, SIM-card scams, fake tour operators on social media, seafood restaurant weight scams
  • Numbeo Crime/Safety Index
    23.35 (Crime Index) / 76.65 (Safety Index) index points (0-100 scale)
  • Real safety concern — motorcycle-traffic risk
    Yes
  • Violent crime
    Extremely rare — one of the safest cities in Vietnam/SE Asia, well-patrolled tourist areas
